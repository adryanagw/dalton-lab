# Bab 2 Ekonomi — Latihan Soal PDF companion spec

Second, separate PDF book (bab id `bab2-pendapatan-nasional-latihan`), distinct from the main
content companion (`bab2-pendapatan-nasional.pdf-spec.md`). 22 original practice questions,
written & solved independently by the Dalton Lab team.

**Structure:** questions-only pages first (no inline answers), so a student can attempt every
question before checking anything — then all 22 answers + explanations collected together on the
final "Kunci Jawaban" page(s). Each question's stem and its five options each get their own line
(a real spacing bug in the first draft ran the stem and "A. ... B. ... C. ..." together on one line).

**Attribution (internal note, not shown to students):** originally based on two Zenius Education
practice sheets (`cgc-4406`, `cgc-5589`) supplied for this chapter — their answer key was behind an
inaccessible URL/QR code, so every question here was independently solved & verified by the Dalton
Lab team, not copied from any key. Rendered pages and quiz.json no longer name Zenius or the file IDs;
questions are presented as Dalton Lab's own, numbered continuously 1-22.

## Page 8 — Kunci Jawaban (ref-list-2col, items 1-11)
```
01 — Jawaban: B — Nilai total hasil produksi barang &amp; jasa diukur berdasarkan nilai uangnya (moneter) — bukan kas negara, bukan penerimaan pajak, dan harus barang/jasa akhir (bukan barang mentah).
02 — Jawaban: E (1.850) — NNP=25.700&minus;3.200=22.500. NNI=22.500&minus;1.200=21.300. PI=NNI+TP&minus;(Laba Ditahan+Pajak Perseroan+Iuran Asuransi) &rarr; 20.250=21.300+TP&minus;2.900 &rarr; TP=1.850.
03 — Jawaban: C (180) — Jumlah nilai tambah tiap sektor: 100+20+30+30=180, bukan total output (550, double counting) atau total input (370).
04 — Jawaban: B (Balada) — Per kapita: Intan&asymp;1.263, Swadikap=1.250, Sejahtera&asymp;1.233, Balada&asymp;1.294 (tertinggi), Juara&asymp;1.186 (ribu US$/jiwa).
05 — Jawaban: B (10.710) — GNP=13.800, NNP=13.560, NNI=12.060, PI=11.510, DI=10.710 (langkah lengkap ada di materi PDF utama, Halaman 5).
06 — Jawaban: E (73jt) — Abaikan 4 item pendekatan pendapatan (Upah+Laba+Bunga+Sewa=35jt, itu jebakan opsi A). Jumlahkan 5 item sisanya: Pengeluaran RT(36jt)+Konsumsi(25jt)+Pengeluaran pemerintah(10jt)+Ekspor(7jt)&minus;Impor(5jt)=Rp73.000.000.
07 — Jawaban: B — Gini 0 = pemerataan sempurna, Kurva Lorenz berimpit garis diagonal.
08 — Jawaban: A (Kep. Bangka Belitung) — Gini terkecil (0,262) di antara keenamnya.
09 — Jawaban: E — Y=r+w+i+p. "Nilai tambah" (D) itu ciri pendekatan produksi, bukan pendapatan.
10 — Jawaban: C — PDRB adalah versi GDP khusus wilayah sub-nasional (provinsi/kabupaten).
11 — Jawaban: A (0,4&ndash;0,5) — Sepenuhnya di dalam kategori Sedang (0,30&ndash;0,50), tidak menyerempet Rendah/Tinggi seperti opsi lain.
```

## Page 9 — Kunci Jawaban (lanjutan) (ref-list-2col, items 12-22)
```
12 — Jawaban: C (PDRB) — Definisi PDRB persis ini.
13 — Jawaban: E (113.500) — Y=37.500+46.400+21.500+(14.500&minus;6.400)=113.500.
14 — Jawaban: E — Disposable Income = pendapatan siap dibelanjakan/ditabung.

17 — Jawaban: D (2,3,6) — Investasi, Konsumsi RT, Konsumsi pemerintah. 1,4,5 itu pendekatan pendapatan.
```
